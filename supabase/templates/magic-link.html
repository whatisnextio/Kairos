Welcome to 12K.

12K means 12 weeks powered by Kairos. Kairos means the right moment: the point where attention becomes action.

Over 84 days, the app helps you choose simple actions across four areas: Body, Fuel, Self, and Connection. Each day asks for one useful action. Then you mark what happened: Done, Part done, or Missed.

The four areas

Body for movement, sleep, and physical energy. Fuel for food, drink, and choices that affect energy. Self for attention, recovery, confidence, and reflection. Connection for family, friends, partners, and practical support.

Your first link opens the app so you can set your four starter actions and begin. No perfect-day pressure, just a clear next step.
