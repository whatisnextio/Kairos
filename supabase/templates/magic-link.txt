Welcome to 12K.

12K means 12 weeks powered by Kairos. Kairos means the right moment: the point where attention becomes action.

Over 84 days, the app helps you choose simple actions across four areas:
- Body: movement, sleep, and physical energy.
- Fuel: food, drink, and choices that affect energy.
- Self: attention, recovery, confidence, and reflection.
- Connection: family, friends, partners, and practical support.

Each day asks for one useful action. Then you mark what happened: Done, Part done, or Missed.

Your first link opens the app so you can set your four starter actions and begin. No perfect-day pressure, just a clear next step.
