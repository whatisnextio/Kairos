Welcome to 12K.

12K means 12 weeks powered by the Kairos framework. Kairos means the right moment: the point where intention becomes action, and action becomes visible change. Twelve weeks is long enough to prove change to yourself, and short enough to keep the work visible.

Over 84 days, the framework helps you build proof across four areas that shape a life:
- Body: movement and physical standards.
- Fuel: food, drink, sleep, and energy.
- Self: attention, recovery, confidence, and identity.
- Connection: the people and relationships that keep life real.

Each day asks for the smallest useful action, then records what actually happened so change becomes something you can see, not just something you meant to do.

Your first link opens the app so you can start the Kickoff phase, choose your first focus, and begin the daily proof loop. No perfect-day pressure, just a clear next step.
