# Kairos / 12K Framework Brief

This is the canonical product brief for 12K and the Kairos framework. Product copy, onboarding, AI prompts, support content, investor copy, and marketing should stay aligned to this page unless the framework itself changes.

## Plain-English Position

12K is a guided 12-week reset powered by Kairos. It is not a 12-step programme and the K is not a forced acronym. The product uses 84 days because it creates a finite arc long enough for repetition, recovery, and visible change without becoming an open-ended habit tracker.

Kairos is the source concept. Kairos means the right, fitting, critical, or opportune moment in Greek. In the app, it means the moment where attention becomes action.

The product promise is:

> 12 weeks. Four domains. Daily proof.

The app should feel like a daily operating system:

1. choose the next useful move,
2. do the smallest honest version,
3. check in,
4. recover quickly if the day slips,
5. review the flywheel weekly,
6. finish the 84-day cycle deliberately.

## Public Framework

The public framework has four domains for everyone:

| Domain | Covers | Product role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Movement, sleep, recovery, strength, physical energy | Make the body harder to ignore and easier to restart. |
| Fuel | Food, water, alcohol, caffeine, energy choices | Reduce avoidable drag and make the next choice easier. |
| Self | Attention, recovery, reflection, identity, mood, confidence | Regulate the person so the other choices become possible. |
| Connection | Partner, family, friends, affection, communication, support | Build reliable presence without pressure or explicit labels. |

Personal routes sit under the public framework. Examples: photography under Self, work or SHOT under Connection or Self depending on intent, money under Fuel as a foundational resource route, family/Nest under Connection. The framework should not become "Liam's categories" for every user.

## The 84-Day Arc

12K is six 14-day phases:

| Phase | Days | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kickoff | 1-14 | Start small and create the first visible wins. |
| Anchor | 15-28 | Make the behaviour easy to find, repeat, and protect. |
| Increase | 29-42 | Add controlled load without breaking the floor. |
| Rhythm | 43-56 | Turn good days into a repeatable weekly pattern. |
| Own | 57-70 | Remove friction and make the behaviour feel like yours. |
| Sustain | 71-84 | Hold the gain and choose the next cycle deliberately. |

The phases should appear early in onboarding, in progress, in AI context, and in Day 84 reflection. They should not be hidden only on a progress page.

## Behaviour Model

Kairos is a practical behaviour-change wrapper, not a clinical programme. It uses evidence-aligned ingredients without making medical claims:

- clear goals and implementation intentions,
- prompts and cues,
- self-monitoring,
- feedback,
- repetition,
- positive reinforcement,
- recovery after lapses,
- social support where squad features exist.

The core recovery rule is: a missed action is not a failed day. The app should offer a smaller catch-up path and let Partial record a smaller useful version.

## AI and Nudges

AI should act as the framework voice. It should use:

- identity anchor,
- current phase,
- selected domains and personal routes,
- recent check-ins,
- streaks and slips,
- notification intensity,
- notes only where the user provided them.

AI should not become open-ended therapy, diagnosis, medical advice, legal advice, or financial advice. The nudge should normally produce one next action, one recovery option, or one reflection prompt.

## Privacy Language

Labels must be discreet on lock screens and public phone views. Self can include private check-ins. Connection can include partner closeness, but should stay comfort-first, consent-led, and non-explicit. Public users should see normal terms: Body, Fuel, Self, Connection. Private coded labels can exist where the user chooses them.

## IP and Naming Posture

This is a product and brand note, not legal advice.

The safer posture is:

- Do claim ownership of our original software, copy, designs, logo files, brand lock-up, generated assets we own, data structures, prompts, and written expression of the 12K/Kairos system.
- Do use Kairos as a meaningful source concept connected to timing and action.
- Do not claim ownership of the ancient Greek word "Kairos" itself.
- Do not say Kairos is Latin or that it means "implement".
- Do not imply copyright protects the underlying idea, system, method, or habit-change concept.
- Do not treat a quick public database search as formal trade mark clearance.

Potentially protectable commercial identifiers:

- 12K,
- Kairos 12K,
- the Kairos/12K logo lock-up,
- "12 weeks. Four domains. Daily proof.",
- specific product copy and visual identity,
- the particular named 84-day phase expression where distinctive enough.

Risk notes:

- "Kairos" is a crowded ancient/common term, so clearance risk is likely higher than for a coined mark.
- "12K" is shorter and more distinctive in this category, but it still needs a proper clearance search before major spend.
- Formal review should include UK, US, EU, WIPO Global Brand Database, app stores, domains, Companies House, social handles, and similar wellness/coaching/software classes.
